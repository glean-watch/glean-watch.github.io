Vecka

Weekly Activity Chart

Utility

Get it on the Connect IQ Store

Your week as seven bars: every day against its goal, last week's days still to come, the week's total and average, and weekly intensity minutes round the rim.

Vecka shows your week the way your watch has been keeping it: seven bars, one a day, from the first day of the week your watch is set to. A day that met its goal is lit in the accent, a day that fell short is dim, and today is bright and still growing. A dashed line marks the goal.

The days still to come show last week's same days in outline, so there is always something to beat. Under the bars, each day's initial in your watch's language; under those, the week's total, the daily average and how many days met their goal so far.

Above the chart, today's figure against its goal, the time in Space Grotesk and the date with the ISO week number. Round the rim, your weekly intensity minutes against Garmin's weekly goal, with the figure where the ring begins. Battery and alerts sit at the top.

The bars can show steps, calories, distance, floors or the day's intensity minutes. Five themes: Ink, Coral, Forest, Mono and Paper. On memory-in-pixel screens every theme is drawn for 64 colours. The always-on mode keeps the time and the week's bars in outline.
