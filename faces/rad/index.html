The face on the watch

The time on one line in large figures in the style of road signs, with the date and two readings in large type under it: all of it readable without glasses.

Rad sets the time on a single line, as wide as the round screen allows at its height, in condensed figures in the style of road signs: lettering made to be read at a distance and at speed. The hours are white, the colon and the minutes the colour of your theme.

Around it, the things you check every day are large too. The date sits above the time in your watch's own language, battery and alerts above it. Under the time, two readings stand in rounded chips, each with its icon: heart rate and steps to start, or the weather, Body Battery, calories, distance, sunrise and sunset or any of fifteen sources. The chips size themselves to what they hold. Below them a short bar fills as your steps meet their goal, or floors, intensity minutes, Body Battery, battery or stress.

Six themes in the colours of road signs: Yellow, Green, Blue, Orange, White and Paper, dark figures on a white ground for anyone who reads better that way. 12 and 24 hour time, without a leading zero in 12 hour. The always-on mode keeps the time at the same size in a light weight.
