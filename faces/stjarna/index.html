The face on the watch

Your zodiac constellation in real stars, lit by your Body Battery, with the moon in its real phase.

Stjärna puts your sign in the sky. Its constellation is drawn from the real stars, each at its true place and brightness, under a fine zodiac ring with your sign's twelfth of it picked out. The sign follows the sun through the year, or keep your own all year round in the settings.

The stars are your Body Battery. Rested, the whole figure is lit and joined in gold; as the day spends your energy the stars dim one by one, in the order the figure is drawn. The brightest stars are the largest, and the brightest of all carries a small glint.

Beside the date, in your watch's own language, the moon shows its real phase, from a sliver to full and back. Battery and alerts sit beneath, the time in Josefin Sans, and two readings at the bottom: heart rate and steps to start with.

Five skies: midnight blue and gold, amethyst, rosewood, onyx and silver, and lagoon. The stars can follow Body Battery, steps, stress, intensity minutes, floors or battery.

On memory-in-pixel screens every sky is drawn for 64 colours; on AMOLED the stars are anti-aliased. The always-on mode keeps the figure's lines, its lit stars, the moon's lit edge and the time.
