Logg

Terminal Data Readout

Geek

Get it on the Connect IQ Store

Your watch as a terminal: seven lines of data, each with a bar towards its goal or a two-hour sparkline, under the time in a monospace face.

Logg turns your watch into a terminal. Seven lines run down one column, each a three-letter key, a value and something to read it by: a bar of ten cells for anything with a goal, filling as you go, or the last two hours as a sparkline for heart rate, barometric pressure and elevation.

Every line can show any of fifteen sources: heart rate, steps, Body Battery, stress, pressure, elevation, the weather, calories, distance, floors, intensity minutes, the next sunrise or sunset, notifications or battery, or nothing at all. Lower lines hold fewer cells, so nothing runs off the round screen.

Above them, the time in JetBrains Mono, the day in your watch's own language with the date written the ISO 8601 way, and battery and alerts. Below them, a prompt and a steady block cursor; it does not blink, because a blink would wake the watch every second.

Five themes: Phosphor, Amber, Ice, Paper and Plasma. On memory-in-pixel screens every theme is drawn for 64 colours. The always-on mode keeps the time and the prompt.
