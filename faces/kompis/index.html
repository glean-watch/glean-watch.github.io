The face on the watch

A little pet that lives on your watch: its hearts fill with your steps, and its mood follows your Body Battery.

Kompis is a small round creature with pointed ears and a sprout on its head, and it lives on your watch. Five hearts sit under it, and they are your step goal: each heart is a fifth of the goal, filling half a heart at a time. When all five are full, Kompis throws its arms up and confetti falls.

Its mood is your Body Battery. Rested, it bounces with its eyes shut tight and its mouth wide open; as the day goes on it is happy, then tired, with heavy eyelids, and worn out, with a drop of sweat, when your battery runs low. At bedtime it goes to sleep, from the sleep time in your Garmin profile to your wake time, and wakes up with you. Sleep can be turned off.

The time is set large in Baloo 2, a round and friendly typeface, with the date above it in your watch's own language. Two readings sit beside the pet, heart rate and steps to start with, and battery and alerts at the foot.

Five colours of Kompis: mint, peach, berry, sky and lemon. The hearts can fill with steps, Body Battery, intensity minutes, floors or battery instead, and the mood can follow any of them but stress.

On memory-in-pixel screens Kompis is drawn for 64 colours; on AMOLED it is anti-aliased. The always-on mode shows Kompis asleep, as a fine outline, and the time.
