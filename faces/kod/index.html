The face on the watch

The time as a code change: the minute just gone removed on a red line, now added on a green one, with your data as bullet lines below.

Kod shows the time the way a developer reads changes: as a diff. Under a hunk header carrying the date, the minute that has just gone is removed on a red line and the current one is added on a green one, larger, in Geist Mono. It follows your 12 or 24 hour setting, so at the top of the hour 11:00 removes 10:59.

Below, three bullet lines, each any of fifteen sources: heart rate, steps and the weather to start, or calories, distance, floors, intensity minutes, Body Battery, stress, pressure, elevation, the next sunrise or sunset, notifications or battery. A source with a goal adds a five-cell stat of plus and minus signs, and its bullet turns green when the goal is met.

Under a prompt with a steady cursor, your Body Battery reads as the context you have left: or battery, steps, floors, intensity minutes or stress, or nothing. Battery and alerts sit at the top.

Five themes: Graphite, Paper (a light editor), Midnight, Mono and Neon. On memory-in-pixel screens every theme is drawn for 64 colours. The always-on mode keeps the added minute and the prompt.
