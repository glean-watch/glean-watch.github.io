The face on the watch

A cherry branch that blooms with your Body Battery, under a sky that follows the sun.

Blom is a branch of cherry blossom across the sky. Its flowers are your Body Battery: rested, every one of the eighteen is open; as the day spends your energy they close from the tips inward, and each one that closes lets a petal fall onto the grass. You read it at a glance, and a small legend underneath says what the blossom is measuring and how far along it is.

The garden holds four readings for a calmer day: stress, heart rate, the weather, and the next sunrise or sunset, each marked by its icon. Battery and alerts sit with the date at the top.

The sky follows the sun: dawn after sunrise, a soft blue day, dusk before sunset and a starry night after it, when the blossom turns silver. Or keep one of five skies: dawn, day, dusk, night or blush.

The numerals are Fraunces, a soft serif. On memory-in-pixel screens every sky is drawn for 64 colours, dithered by hand; on AMOLED it is a smooth gradient. The always-on mode keeps the branch, its open flowers and the time.
