The face on the watch

The time in figures as tall as your watch allows, hours over minutes, so you can read it at arm's length without your glasses.

Stor puts the time in the largest figures a round screen can hold: the hours on one line, the minutes on the next, each nearly a third of the screen's height. Heavy, open figures in a modern grotesque, white on black, with the minutes in a colour of your choice, read at a glance across a room, in the car or first thing in the morning before your glasses are on.

Everything else stays out of the way. A thin bar between the two lines fills as your steps meet their goal, so progress needs no reading at all; it can follow floors, intensity minutes, Body Battery, battery or stress instead, or be switched off. Battery and alerts sit at the top. The date sits at the foot in your watch's own language, or put one reading there instead: heart rate, the weather, steps or any of fifteen sources.

Six themes: Coral, Amber, Mint, Sky, Mono and Paper, dark figures on a white ground for anyone who reads better that way, and for a bright day on a memory-in-pixel screen. 12 and 24 hour time, without a leading zero in 12 hour. The always-on mode keeps the same figures at the same size in a hairline weight, large enough to read in the dark.
