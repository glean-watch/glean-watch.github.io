The face on the watch

Four sensors' last hours as charts, one above the other: heart rate, Body Battery, stress and pressure, each with its high and low.

Kurva puts the history your watch has been recording all along on the dial. Four rows, one sensor each: its icon, its value now, its last hours drawn as an area under a line, and the high and the low of that span.

Every chart sits in the same columns, so all four share one clock. A morning run is a peak in heart rate, a step down in Body Battery and a gap in stress, one above the other. Where the watch took no reading the chart leaves a gap; it never draws a guess as a zero.

Each row can show heart rate, Body Battery, stress, barometric pressure or elevation, and the charts reach back 2, 4, 8 or 12 hours. Two more readings sit at the bottom, from fifteen sources: steps, calories, distance, floors, intensity minutes, the weather, the next sunrise or sunset and more. The time is set large in Inter Tight, with the date in your watch's own language, and battery and alerts at the top.

Each sensor keeps its own colour in every row. Five themes: Night, Slate, Paper, Mono and Amber. On memory-in-pixel screens every theme is drawn for 64 colours. The always-on mode keeps the time and the four lines.
