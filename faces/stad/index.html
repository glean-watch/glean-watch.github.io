The face on the watch

A game's HUD over a city map: five stars for your stress, your steps as cash, health and armour bars, and an arrow that heads for the minute.

Stad turns your watch into the heads-up display of an open-world game. The whole dial is a map of an invented city: blocks between wide streets, a park, a boulevard, a highway sweeping round the west side, a beach and the sea.

Over it, everything you need, outlined in black so it reads on any street. Five stars across the top fill with your stress. The time sits in the middle in Big Shoulders Display, with your steps under it as cash: 8,420 steps is $8,420. Two arcs along the bottom are your health and armour, Body Battery and battery by default. The player's arrow stands below the centre and turns to point at the minute, and two blips on the map carry any reading you like.

Every gauge can be changed: the stars and the bars follow steps, floors, intensity minutes, Body Battery, battery or stress; the cash is steps, calories, floors or intensity minutes; the blips take any of fifteen sources, the weather and heart rate to start. The date is in your watch's own language, with battery and alerts below the arrow.

Five themes: Street, Night, Sunset, Atlas (a light map) and Mono. The always-on mode keeps the time in a light cut and the arrow's outline.

Touch and hold a blip, the cash, the stars or a bar to open your watch's own glance for what it shows, the date for your calendar. The seconds tick on a plate beside the time, or switch them off. Temperatures follow your watch, or pick °C or °F.
