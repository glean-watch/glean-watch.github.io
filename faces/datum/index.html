The face on the watch

Twelve live readings around a large, clear time, every one of them yours.

Everything at a glance, and nothing decorative. A large condensed time, hours in white and minutes in your accent colour, with the date and live seconds beneath it. Around it, six data fields you choose, each marked by its icon, and two bezel gauges for your steps, battery, Body Battery, floors, intensity minutes or stress, with their readings under each arc.

Weather and the next sunrise or sunset are among the sources. Battery, notifications, do-not-disturb and alarm sit at the bottom, shown only when they have something to say.

Six accent colours. The always-on mode keeps the time and the date.

Its numerals are cut for each screen size, never scaled.

Touch and hold a field or a bezel gauge to open your watch's own glance for it, the date for your calendar. Temperatures follow your watch, or pick °C or °F.
