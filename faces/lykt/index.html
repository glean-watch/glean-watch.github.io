Lykt

Jack-o'-Lantern

Fun

Get it on the Connect IQ Store

The whole dial is a carved pumpkin, and every hole in it has a job.

Lykt is a jack-o'-lantern, and the watch is the light inside it. The time is the grin: its digits are the teeth left uncut, dark against the candle. Each eye holds a reading, heart rate and steps to start with, and the nose is your Body Battery: the candle fills it from the base, a dark nose when you are spent and a nose full of light when you are rested. Battery and alerts sit under the lid; the date is on the skin below the grin.

Five pumpkins: classic orange, a white ghost pumpkin with a cold blue glow, ember red, poison green and a black midnight pumpkin with an orange glow.

The numerals are Oswald, tall and narrow like carved teeth. On memory-in-pixel screens every pumpkin is drawn for 64 colours; on AMOLED the glow is brightest at its heart. The always-on mode keeps the carved outlines, the candle's level and the time, lit in the dark.
