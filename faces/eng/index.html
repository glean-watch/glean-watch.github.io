Eng

Nature

Get it on the Connect IQ Store

A lavender field that flowers with your Body Battery, under a sky that follows the sun.

Eng is a meadow of lavender running to the hills. Its bloom is your Body Battery: rested, the rows are in flower all the way to the horizon; as the day spends your energy the colour goes back down the field and the rows return to bud. You read it the way you read a real field, and a small legend underneath says what the bloom is measuring and how far it has come.

The foreground holds four readings for a calmer day: stress, heart rate, the weather, and the next sunrise or sunset, each marked by its icon. Battery and alerts sit with the date at the top.

The sky follows the sun: dawn after sunrise, a clear blue day, the lavender hour before sunset, and a night with fireflies over the field. Or keep one of five skies: dawn, day, dusk, night or golden.

The numerals are Fraunces, a soft serif. On memory-in-pixel screens every sky is drawn for 64 colours, dithered by hand; on AMOLED it is a smooth gradient. The always-on mode keeps the meadow's crest, the edge of the bloom and the time.
