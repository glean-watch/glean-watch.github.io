The face on the watch

An analog face for reading without glasses: twelve big numerals, a broad white hour hand and a long minute hand in colour that you will never mix up.

Visare is an analog watch face made to be read without glasses. Twelve big numerals in the style of road signs stand round the dial, white on black, as large as the round screen holds them, with sixty minute marks at the edge.

The two hands cannot be taken for each other. The hour hand is short, broad and white; the minute hand is long, slimmer and the colour of your theme. Each has a thin outline in the colour of the dial, so it stands out clearly wherever it crosses a numeral or a reading. A slim seconds hand with a round counterweight ticks or sweeps, or stays off; on memory-in-pixel screens it keeps running with your wrist down. Turn on adaptive hands and a hand opens to an outline where it crosses a reading, so nothing is ever hidden.

Inside the numerals, the day and date in your watch's own language above the centre, and two readings with their icons, one above the centre and one below: heart rate and battery to start, or steps, the weather, Body Battery, sunrise and sunset or any of twenty sources, VO2 max and sleep score among them, or none. A small digital time can go under them, in 12-hour or 24-hour or as your watch is set, with or without a leading zero.

Prefer a calmer dial? Show only 12, 3, 6 and 9 with bold bars between, or bars all round. Six themes in the colours of road signs: Yellow, Green, Blue, Orange, White and Paper, black numerals on a light dial for anyone who reads better that way. The always-on mode keeps the numerals and both hands at full size in a light weight.

Touch and hold a reading to open its glance on your watch, or the date for your calendar. Temperatures follow your watch, or pick °C or °F.
