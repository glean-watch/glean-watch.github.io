Help

Installing a face

  1. On your phone, open the Connect IQ Store app and check that your watch is the selected device.
  2. Find the face, from its page here or by searching for its name, and choose Install.
  3. Keep the watch near the phone while it syncs. The face appears on the watch when the download finishes.

Every face's page here has a button that opens it in the store.

Changing a face's settings

Settings are changed on the phone, not on the watch. In the Connect IQ Store app, open your watch's installed watch faces, choose the face, then Settings. Save, and the watch takes the change at its next sync.

Questions

Why does the weather show --?

A face draws the weather your watch last received through Garmin Connect on your phone. Until the watch has a reading, the face shows -- rather than make one up.

Why do sunrise and sunset show --?

Sun times need to know where you are. The faces use the place of the watch's weather reading, then the position of your last recorded activity. Before either exists they show -- rather than guess.

Does a face need my phone?

Only to install it, to change its settings and to bring the weather. Everything else is drawn by the watch on its own.

Is it free?

Yes, every Glean face is free on the Connect IQ Store. If one has earned its place on your wrist, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi. It's never expected.
