Refunds

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Unlock codes are not refundable once delivered.

Every Glean face can be tried in full before you pay: its free trial edition runs for 2 days on your own watch, so you can see that it works and that you like it. The code is delivered the moment the payment goes through. By buying it, you ask for it to be delivered at once and accept that this ends the 14-day right of withdrawal that consumers in the EU and the UK otherwise have for digital content.

If a payment is refunded or reversed all the same, by Paddle, our reseller, or by your bank, the code stops working: the face goes back to its locked trial state the next time the watch syncs with its phone.

A new watch

A code works on one watch. If that watch is replaced because it broke or was lost, you can have a code for the new watch free of charge: send your order number (it is on your Paddle receipt) and your new watch ID, which the face shows under Watch ID in its settings, to Glean on Facebook.

Nothing on this page limits the rights you have by law where you live.
