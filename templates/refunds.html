Refunds

Last updated 22 November 2026.

If you are not happy with an unlock code, ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying it. You do not need to give a reason.

Ask through the link in the receipt Paddle emailed you, or at paddle.net, where you can find your order with the email address you paid with. Paddle, our reseller, handles every refund. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with.

A refunded code stops working: the face goes back to its locked trial state the next time the watch syncs with its phone, within a day.

A new watch

A code works on one watch. If that watch is replaced because it broke or was lost, you can have a code for the new watch free of charge: send your order number (it is on your Paddle receipt) and your new watch ID, which the face shows under Watch ID in its settings, to Glean on Facebook.

Paid twice?

If you were charged twice for the same face on the same watch, ask for a refund of the second payment as above, at any time.
