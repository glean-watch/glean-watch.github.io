Installing a face

  1. On your phone, open the Connect IQ Store app and check that your watch is the selected device.
  2. Find the face, from its page here or by searching for its name, and choose Install.
  3. Keep the watch near the phone while it syncs. The face appears on the watch when the download finishes.

Every face's page here has a button that opens it in the store.

Changing a face's settings

Settings are changed on the phone, not on the watch. In the Connect IQ Store app, open your watch's installed watch faces, choose the face, then Settings. Save, and the watch takes the change at its next sync.

Questions

Why does the weather show --?

A face draws the weather your watch last received through Garmin Connect on your phone. Until the watch has a reading, the face shows -- rather than make one up.

Why do sunrise and sunset show --?

Sun times need to know where you are. The faces use the place of the watch's weather reading, then the position of your last recorded activity. Before either exists they show -- rather than guess.

Does a face need my phone?

Only to install it, to change its settings and to bring the weather. Everything else is drawn by the watch on its own.

Is it free?

Most Glean faces, Stargazing Forecast and Gem Crush are a one-time purchase on the Connect IQ Store, paid once and updated from then on; a few faces and One More Step are free. The store shows which is which before you install.

How do I open a reading's glance?

On a touch-screen watch with current firmware, touch and hold a reading on a paid face: the watch opens its own glance for it, the one you would reach by scrolling. Hold the date to open your calendar. Watches without touch, and older firmware, simply show the face.

Can a face show seconds?

Every paid face has a Seconds setting, on by default. On a memory-in-pixel screen they keep ticking while the watch is at rest; on an AMOLED screen they show while the screen is lit, and the always-on screen leaves them out to spare the display.

One More Step

One More Step installs as a face does, from its page in the Connect IQ Store app. It is a widget with a glance: from the watch face, scroll through your glances to find it, and select it to open the app. If it is not among them, add it in the watch's settings under Glances (Widgets on some watches).

Why does it say ETA calculating?

The ETA is the distance left at your pace so far, so it needs one full day first. Until then the ring says so and counts down the hours.

Why is a day missing?

The watch keeps its last 7 days, and One More Step reads them every 6 hours. If it cannot read them for more than 7 days, with the watch switched off for instance, those days are lost: it never guesses in their place.

Why does my ride not count?

Only the distance your watch works out from your steps counts, recorded walks and runs included. Cycling and swimming do not.

Can I take my journey to a new watch?

No. The journey lives on the watch, and removing the app deletes it: watch apps have no backup.

Stargazing Forecast

Stargazing Forecast installs as a face does, from its page in the Connect IQ Store app. It is a widget with a glance: from the watch face, scroll through your glances to find it, and select it to open the app. If it is not among them, add it in the watch's settings under Glances (Widgets on some watches). Your favourite kind of sky, the one the glance shows, is chosen in the app's menu or in its settings on the phone.

Why does it show no verdict?

It needs a forecast, and the forecast comes through your phone. Keep the phone connected and open the app once; after that it fetches a new forecast every three hours and keeps the last one. It also needs to know where you are: the place of the watch's weather reading, your last activity, or one GPS fix taken while the app is open.

Why only Maybe on a clear night?

A Go needs at least an hour of good conditions with the full forecast behind it. When the forecast is old or incomplete, or only cloud cover is known for those hours, the app says Maybe at most rather than promise a sky it cannot see.

Gem Crush

Gem Crush is a watch app: open it from the watch's list of apps and activities, as you would start a run. Turn a ring with a finger on a touch screen, or with the buttons.

Where do charges come from?

Every 1,000 steps you take today is a charge, up to ten a day, and a charge clears a whole ring at once. They start again each day.

Is the daily board the same for everyone?

Yes. One board a day, 25 moves, the same on every watch; keep playing it day after day to build your streak. Free play, 30 moves a round, is there whenever you like.
