Help

Installing a face

  1. On your phone, open the Connect IQ Store app and check that your watch is the selected device.
  2. Find the face, from its page here or by searching for its name, and choose Install.
  3. Keep the watch near the phone while it syncs. The face appears on the watch when the download finishes.

Every face's page here has a button that opens it in the store.

Changing a face's settings

Settings are changed on the phone, not on the watch. In the Connect IQ Store app, open your watch's installed watch faces, choose the face, then Settings. Save, and the watch takes the change at its next sync.

Questions

Why does the weather show --?

A face draws the weather your watch last received through Garmin Connect on your phone. Until the watch has a reading, the face shows -- rather than make one up.

Why do sunrise and sunset show --?

Sun times need to know where you are. The faces use the place of the watch's weather reading, then the position of your last recorded activity. Before either exists they show -- rather than guess.

Does a face need my phone?

Only to install it, to change its settings and to bring the weather. Everything else is drawn by the watch on its own.

Is it free?

Yes, every Glean face and app is free on the Connect IQ Store. If one has earned its place on your wrist, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi. It's never expected.

One More Step

One More Step installs as a face does, from its page in the Connect IQ Store app. It is a widget with a glance: from the watch face, scroll through your glances to find it, and select it to open the app. If it is not among them, add it in the watch's settings under Glances (Widgets on some watches).

Why does it say ETA calculating?

The ETA is the distance left at your pace so far, so it needs one full day first. Until then the ring says so and counts down the hours.

Why is a day missing?

The watch keeps its last 7 days, and One More Step reads them every 6 hours. If it cannot read them for more than 7 days, with the watch switched off for instance, those days are lost: it never guesses in their place.

Why does my ride not count?

Only the distance your watch works out from your steps counts, recorded walks and runs included. Cycling and swimming do not.

Can I take my journey to a new watch?

No. The journey lives on the watch, and removing the app deletes it: watch apps have no backup.
