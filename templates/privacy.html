Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

The watch faces

Glean's faces collect nothing and send nothing. None of them has network access: they never connect to the internet, to a server of mine or to anyone else's. Everything a face shows is read on the watch and drawn on the watch.

A Connect IQ face asks for permissions, and the store lists them. Glean's faces ask for:

Sensor history
Your recent heart rate, Body Battery, stress, pressure and elevation, read on the watch to draw them on the dial.
User profile (one face only)
Your usual sleep and wake times from the watch's settings, read on the watch and nowhere else.

Installing a face and changing its settings go through Garmin's Connect IQ Store and Garmin Connect, under Garmin's own privacy policy.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from any other site: its fonts and pictures are served from here. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs, such as visitors' IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
