Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

The watch faces

Glean's faces collect nothing and send nothing. None of them has network access, except the free trial editions below: they never connect to the internet, to a server of mine or to anyone else's. Everything a face shows is read on the watch and drawn on the watch.

A Connect IQ face asks for permissions, and the store lists them. Glean's faces ask for:

Sensor history
Your recent heart rate, Body Battery, stress, pressure and elevation, read on the watch to draw them on the dial.
User profile (one face only)
Your usual sleep and wake times from the watch's settings, read on the watch and nowhere else.

Installing a face and changing its settings go through Garmin's Connect IQ Store and Garmin Connect, under Garmin's own privacy policy.

Free trial editions and unlock codes

A face's free trial edition asks the Glean key server, through your phone's connection, when the trial began and whether the face has been unlocked. The request carries the face's name and its watch ID: eight letters and digits that this face works out from an identifier the watch gives each app, so it is not your watch's serial number and means nothing to any other app. The server keeps the ID, the face and the day it first saw them, so reinstalling the face does not restart the trial. Nothing else is sent: no name, no place, no health data. The server runs on Cloudflare, which sees the request as any website sees a visit. The trial edition asks every half hour until the server first answers, then every six hours during the trial, every quarter of an hour once the trial is over (so a code bought then unlocks the face within minutes), and once a day after the face is unlocked, so that a refunded purchase locks it again.

It asks for two permissions beyond a face's usual ones:

Communications
The request described above, made through your phone.
Background
A short run on the watch that makes the request while the face is on the dial.

When you buy an unlock code, Paddle, our reseller, takes your payment and email address under the Paddle privacy notice, and tells the key server which face and watch ID were paid for, with the order's number and amount. The key server keeps those, and not your name, email address or card. The paid face and watch ID are what the face asks about to unlock.

One More Step

One More Step collects nothing and sends nothing either, and has no network access. It reads the daily distance your watch already works out from your steps, and keeps your journey, your total and the day it started, in the app's own storage on the watch. Removing the app deletes it.

It asks for one permission:

Background
A short run on the watch every 6 hours that counts the days it has not counted yet, so no day is lost while you leave the app closed.

Stargazing Forecast

Stargazing Forecast is the one Glean app that goes online, and only for the weather. To fetch the forecast for your place, it sends that place, rounded to 0.01 degrees (about a kilometre), to the 7Timer! astronomy forecast service, through your phone's connection, once every three hours and when you open the app. Nothing else is sent: no name, no account, no identifier of you or your watch. The request is an ordinary web request, so 7Timer!'s server sees it as any website sees a visit. The forecast and your last place are kept in the app's own storage on the watch; removing the app deletes them. The Sun, the Moon and the Milky Way are worked out on the watch.

It asks for three permissions:

Positioning
Where you are, for the forecast and the sky. It takes the place of the watch's weather reading first, then of your last activity, and only when the watch knows neither does it ask for one GPS fix while the app is open.
Communications
The forecast request described above, made through your phone.
Background
A short run on the watch every 3 hours that fetches the next forecast, so the glance is up to date without opening the app.

Gem Crush

Gem Crush collects nothing, sends nothing and asks for no permissions. It reads today's steps on the watch to count your charges, and keeps the round you left open, your best scores and your daily streak in the game's own storage on the watch. Removing the game deletes them.

This website

This site sets no cookies, and its fonts and pictures are served from here. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: a small script from Cloudflare reports which page was viewed, the referring site, and the browser, device type and country, without cookies, without storing anything on your device and without telling one visitor from another; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. The other exception is the unlock page, which loads Paddle's checkout from Paddle and asks the key server about the watch ID you type; Paddle's checkout sets its own cookies under Paddle's privacy notice. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard server logs, such as visitors' IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
